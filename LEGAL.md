# PluginDirect — Legal Requirements Before Launch

Last reviewed: June 2026. This document covers UK law requirements for operating a comparison site with affiliate links. **This is not legal advice — consult a solicitor for your specific situation.**

---

## 1. AFFILIATE DISCLOSURE (Required by UK law)

**The law:** The UK Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008 and the ASA/CAP Code require you to clearly disclose when you earn commission from product recommendations.

**What you must do:**
- Add a clear, prominent disclosure on every page (not buried in footer small print)
- Disclose before the first affiliate link, not after
- Say something like: *"PluginDirect earns a commission when you click product links and make a purchase. This doesn't affect our recommendations or the price you pay."*

**Already in the site:** A footer note says "Affiliate links used" — this is not sufficient on its own. Add a visible banner or disclosure block at the top of any page listing products.

**Action required:** Add an affiliate disclosure notice visible above the product comparison section.

---

## 2. PRIVACY POLICY (Required by UK GDPR)

**The law:** UK GDPR (retained from EU GDPR post-Brexit) requires you to have a privacy policy if you collect any personal data. Your site collects:
- Email addresses (calculator capture, exit overlay, price alerts, DNO certificate)
- Postcode (DNO wizard, compliance checker)

**What your privacy policy must cover:**
- What data you collect and why
- How long you keep it
- Who you share it with (email platform, analytics)
- Users' rights (access, deletion, portability)
- Your contact details
- Legal basis for processing (consent or legitimate interest)
- Cookie information

**Action required:** Create a Privacy Policy page. Free generator: [ico.org.uk/for-organisations/guide-to-data-protection/privacy-notice-examples/](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/guide-to-data-protection/privacy-notice-examples/) — but customise it for your actual data flows.

---

## 3. COOKIE CONSENT (Required by PECR)

**The law:** The Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) require user consent before setting non-essential cookies. Google Analytics sets tracking cookies.

**What you must do:**
- Show a cookie consent banner before GA4 loads
- Give users a genuine choice (not just "OK" with no opt-out)
- Record consent

**Easiest free solution:** [Cookieyes](https://www.cookieyes.com) — free tier, PECR compliant, integrates with GA4. Add their script before your GA4 tag.

**Action required:** Add cookie consent before adding GA4 to the site.

---

## 4. INFORMATION COMMISSIONER'S OFFICE (ICO) REGISTRATION

**The law:** Most UK businesses that process personal data must register with the ICO. Cost: £40/year for small organisations.

**Do you need to register?** Yes — you collect email addresses and postcodes.

**Action required:** Register at [ico.org.uk/registration](https://ico.org.uk/registration/new) before collecting your first email address. Fine for non-registration: up to £500.

---

## 5. TERMS & CONDITIONS

**The law:** Not strictly legally required, but strongly recommended. Protects you from liability.

**Your T&Cs should cover:**
- That product prices and availability change and you're not responsible for inaccuracies
- That affiliate links earn you commission
- That the DNO registration wizard is for guidance only, not professional advice
- That solar savings estimates are indicative, not guaranteed
- Limitation of liability
- Governing law (England & Wales)

**Action required:** Create a Terms & Conditions page. [Termly.io](https://termly.io) has a free generator.

---

## 6. FINANCIAL PROMOTION RULES

**The law:** The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 covers financial promotions. Savings estimates and payback projections could be considered financial promotions.

**What this means for you:**
- Savings figures must be realistic and based on reasonable assumptions
- Include a caveat: *"Savings estimates are based on average UK irradiance data and typical usage patterns. Actual savings will vary."*
- Do not guarantee specific savings amounts

**Already partially covered:** The calculator shows "Estimated" figures. Make sure this caveat appears near all savings figures.

**Action required:** Add a visible disclaimer near savings figures. Already partially implemented — review wording.

---

## 7. PRODUCT ACCURACY & CONSUMER RIGHTS ACT 2015

**The law:** Product descriptions must be accurate. If a product is listed with incorrect specifications, price, or claims, you could be liable.

**What you must do:**
- Keep product prices updated (or add "prices correct at time of publication, check retailer for current price")
- Ensure compliance badges are accurate — only mark kits as "G98 Compliant" if they genuinely are
- Don't claim a kit is "UK-certified" without verification

**Action required:** Add "Prices and availability correct at time of publication" near the product grid.

---

## 8. ELECTRICAL SAFETY ADVICE

**The risk:** You provide guidance on electrical installations (the DNO wizard). If someone relies on this advice and something goes wrong, you could face a claim.

**What you must do:**
- Add a disclaimer: *"The DNO registration wizard is for informational guidance only. PluginDirect is not a qualified electrician. Always ensure your installation complies with BS 7671 and consult a qualified electrician if in any doubt."*
- Do not imply the wizard constitutes professional advice

**Action required:** Add disclaimer to the DNO wizard section header.

---

## 9. COMPANIES HOUSE / SOLE TRADER

**The law:** If trading as a business, you must either:
- Register as a **sole trader** with HMRC (free, do it online at gov.uk) — required once you earn income
- Or form a **limited company** at Companies House (£12 online)

**Recommendation:** Start as a sole trader. Register when you make your first £1,000.

**Action required:** Register as sole trader at [gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-sole-trader) when income begins.

---

## 10. VAT

**The law:** You must register for VAT once your turnover exceeds £90,000/year (2026 threshold).

At the revenue projections in this plan, you'll hit this threshold around month 5–6.

**Action required:** Monitor revenue. Register at HMRC when approaching £90,000.
